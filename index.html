The Atelier

The patience of the hand

Every CHRONOVA movement is assembled, regulated and finished by a single watchmaker, who signs the case back before it leaves Genève. Nothing is hidden, so nothing is left unfinished.

  1. I

    Côtes de Genève

    Parallel waves cut across every bridge.

  2. II

    Perlage

    Overlapping circular graining on the main plate.

  3. III

    Anglage

    Bevelled, mirror-polished edges, by hand.
